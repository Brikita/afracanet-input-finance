# AFRACANet AI

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Agricultural lender staff review evidence, authorize allocations, approve input
orders, and reconcile supplier payments. Farmers request inputs and follow their
progress to collection. Agro-dealers manage stock, issue invoices, and record
handover. The lender case view is the starting surface agreed on 26 October 2026.

## Product Purpose

Connect agricultural credit intelligence to authorized input finance, verified
supplier payment, and recorded farmer collection in one understandable journey.

## Positioning

One AFRACANet product spans credit intelligence and execution. The lender owns
credit decisions; the system links evidence and distinct transaction facts.
Commercial differentiation and impact remain hypotheses to validate with partners.

## Operating Context

The credit-intelligence and input-finance repositories remain modular. A trusted
automated approval handoff is planned; current CSV allocation import is manual.
The execution app uses React/TypeScript, FastAPI, and SQLAlchemy. Initial
demonstrations use fictional participants and illustrative USD allocations.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve server-side role and subject checks, integer monetary amounts, atomic
credit/stock reservations, duplicate-event protection, and genuine provider
payment verification. AI recommendations, lender approvals, payment, and
collection are separate facts. Label simulation, public context, synthetic replay,
and provider test modes. Do not claim escrow, guaranteed outcomes, or production
readiness. Provider credentials and live integration checks remain outstanding.

## Brand Commitments

AFRACANet AI is the shared product name. The user requested a full UX redesign
using Taste and Impeccable, followed by meaningful restructuring and comprehensive
synthetic graph/relational data. Preserve factual content and working features.
No replacement visual palette or composition has yet been approved.

## Evidence on Hand

Working execution code and automated journey checks; a small labelled replay
pack in datasets/replay; attributed public weather snapshots in datasets/public.
There is no partner dataset, proven customer impact, or validated predictive
credit model available for claims. Existing upstream example graph data remains
illustrative. Larger synthetic generation is planned.

## Product Principles

- Show who acts next and what evidence supports each decision.
- Keep financial records traceable and amounts reconcilable.
- Explain unavailable and incomplete evidence honestly.
- Treat synthetic histories as software scenarios rather than real loan outcomes.
- Make the whole product recognizable while giving each role a focused task flow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support mobile farmer tasks, keyboard operation, visible focus, clear errors,
reduced motion, and understandable English/Swahili assistance. Full application
translation and real handset delivery require further work and verification.
