## Separate Gemini setup

Set CREDIT_AI_PROVIDER=gemini, GEMINI_API_KEY and
CREDIT_RECOMMENDATION_MODEL=gemini-3.5-flash-lite in this repository's ignored
.env. Never copy another module's credentials. To read a few synthetic cases and
call the actual provider without creating decisions:

```powershell
.venv/Scripts/python.exe scripts/check_credit_ai.py --limit 3
```

The full local output remains ignored in data/evaluations. Schema, citation and
numeric consistency checks do not validate lending accuracy or real outcomes.
