## Direction contract

THESIS: A lender sees where credit stands, which request needs attention, and
who acts next. A connected allocation graphic and selected case replace the
previous promotional banner and repeated summary cards.

OWN-WORLD: Deep teal header, a pale blue ground, white working surfaces, teal
actions, blue reservations, and leaf-green paid amounts. DM Sans for text,
Manrope for headings and values; Lucide outline icons identify real actions.

STORY: Read the current scoped allocation, find a request, inspect its item quote
and separate transaction stages, then take the action permitted by its state.
Keep demonstration and provider modes visible. Upstream evidence remains labelled
unavailable until connected.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-width teal navigation, a concise title and export action,
four plain numerical summaries, then a broad credit graphic and searchable queue
beside a narrow selected-case panel. The primary case action stays visible on
desktop. Tablet stacks the case below the queue; phone uses one column, compact
navigation, and accessible controls.

FORM: Waterline, fifth grounded direction in the second round; seed:0bf6610d1698eecb.
Its signature is a reconciled credit-destination bar paired with a four-stage
request-to-collection path. State changes update the same case and graphic;
reduced motion preserves all information.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Product boundaries

The selected sketch uses synthetic development-pack figures for composition.
The working app renders its own scoped API data, which may be much smaller.
No generated financial histories are silently imported. Invoice status alone
never establishes payment, and a credit recommendation never creates an allocation.
